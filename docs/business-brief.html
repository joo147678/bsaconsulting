General Assembly Minutes Studio

Working name: نظام محاضر الجمعيات العمومية · Arabic only · Internal BSA tool

The bet

BSA already prepares general assembly minutes for at least 100 client companies. Today that work lives in Word: slow, easy to get wrong, too many back-and-forths. This studio lets a BSA accountant draft the محضر once, on a live A4 page that follows Companies Law and BSA house style, then send it to a supervisor for one review. We are building this flow to prove to the client that the operation takes less time and fewer iterations. If that deal closes, this is one BSA service that can scale across the rest of the book.

Who uses it

  • BSA accountant — daily user. Creates the draft from the company register.
  • BSA supervisor — reviews and approves. Not a client login in this product; the client receives the finished minute.

The review flow we must show

Accountant creates a draft → supervisor is notified on WhatsApp with a review link → opening the link marks the minute in review → supervisor clicks Approved. Arabic UI only. Current layout is acceptable for v1; legal wording must still match Companies Law and BSA house style.

This scope vs next

  • Now: UI and UX of the full flow — screens, status, copy — so a demo can replace Word for next month’s client minutes.
  • Next: real roles, cloud, and live WhatsApp. Do not build accounts or filing in this pass.

Success

Next month, BSA issues client minutes from this tool, not Word. The demo must make the time-saved / fewer-iterations story obvious in one sitting.
